# Labels v2: inventory, gold-free consensus tables and image-teacher blends (2026-10-15)

CPU-only label work for the fine-tuning lane. Every table here is built **without the gold-58 labels** (parameter-free means, non-gold quantile maps, Dawid-Skene EM and teacher weights fitted on the 4,349 non-gold studies only); gold-58 is used once, to score. Script and all outputs (ignored): `outputs/labels/labels-v2-20261015/` (`build_labels_v2.py` sha256 `66d93e59324f9459…`, `summary.json` `81719808c9243bd0…`, `inventory.csv`, `candidates.csv`, `per_finding.csv`, label tables below). Bootstrap: 2,000 study-level draws, seed 20261008, shared draws for paired differences. Aggregates only; no report text, IDs or label rows in this doc.

## Verdict

1. **Report-only consensus is saturated.** No gold-free blend of the report tables beats `labels_v1_blend` (what our models trained on) on gold-58: best is rank-mean of five labellers at 0.899 vs 0.901 (paired −0.002 [−0.012, +0.007]); mean-of-6 0.897; Dawid-Skene 0.883 (3-state discretisation loses the soft information); hard majority-with-abstention 0.836. Consensus inherits the majority's systematic errors, most visibly on Fracture, where the steven lineage (0.79) outvotes gemlow (0.90).
2. **An image teacher that never saw the study does add information.** Our own CoAtNet-224 five-fold OOF predictions alone score **0.900** on gold-58 (image-only, 5 fold models that never trained on gold). Blending the report consensus with this teacher gives **`labels_v2_base_own224_w30`: gold-58 macro 0.920 [0.890, 0.945]**, paired **+0.019 [+0.005, +0.034] vs `labels_v1_blend`** (P>0 0.996), **+0.027 [+0.012, +0.043] vs steven_v4** and **+0.016 [+0.001, +0.033] vs jev_hybrid** (the best public table, 0.904, gold-informed). Gold-74 confirms the direction (+0.015 [+0.004, +0.026] vs `labels_v1_blend`). The gain is concentrated on image-visible findings the reports under-call: Effusion +0.099 [+0.041, +0.174], Synovitis +0.041, Lateral Meniscus +0.038, Medial OA +0.034, Contusion +0.025, Medial Meniscus +0.019; it costs a little on ACL, Lateral OA, PF OA and Fracture (−0.003 to −0.017, CIs include 0).
3. **The Dread K42 teacher cannot be evaluated**: its npz has zero gold rows (4,345 = 4,349 non-gold − 4 quarantined) and Dread trained on every non-gold study, so its predictions echo its training labels (exposed agreement with the report consensus 0.95–0.99 AUC, Synovitis 0.85). Its raw outputs are not calibrated (positive fraction 0.17–0.45 vs consensus 0.06–0.59 per finding). The same applies to the A′ members: they trained on all train studies, so the ensemble-teacher job (section 6) yields in-sample echoes, not a validated teacher, unless a fold split can be reconstructed.
4. **Silence is informative, per finding.** On gold, cells where steven or pilkwang is silent are almost always negative for ACL, MCL, Medial/Lateral OA, Effusion, Baker's and Contusion (0–7 % positive vs 18–62 % when mentioned), but **34 % positive for Synovitis (41/58 silent), 20 % for PF OA, 14 % for Fracture and 13 % for Lateral Meniscus**. So "unmentioned = negative" is wrong for those four; replacing silent cells with the OOF teacher scores 0.911 (+0.013 vs base; Fracture +0.082, PF OA +0.037). Masking is the safer default only where the teacher is unavailable.

Caveats that bound these claims are in section 7; the headline gain is a label-quality result on 58 studies, not a model result.

## 5. Teacher leakage and exposure

- **Dread v4_full K42** (`outputs/analysis/raptor64-20261013/it2/teacher-dread-v4-k42-private.npz`, sha256 `dab0621a2302dc46…`, 4,345 ids, 0 gold rows; receipt says "Dread saw all non-gold studies"; Dread's own label table has 4,349 rows, so gold labels were not in its training set, but gold images and gold-58 as a selection set are unverified). Exposed agreement with the base on its own training rows: AUC 0.95–0.99 per finding except Synovitis 0.854; Pearson with own OOF 0.64–0.88. On the 16 JEV-verified extras it scores 0.899 against report-verified labels (report tables 0.976–0.988, own OOF 0.892). Conclusion: unscorable on gold and largely an echo of its training labels; kept only as calibrated reference columns.
- **Own OOF teachers**: val rows are out-of-fold; gold rows are predicted by five models that never trained on gold. Residual exposure: checkpoint selection used report folds (not gold), and the own224/convnext recipes were developed while gold-58 was being looked at (design exposure, per `coatnet224-fullfive-20261010.md`). Gold rows use a 5-fold mean while non-gold rows carry single-fold values; single-fold teacher-only gold scores average 0.889 (vs 0.900 for the mean), but single-fold **blends** average 0.9192 vs 0.9202, so the saved table's non-gold rows should be of the scored quality.
- **A′ members** (nartaa R384/R224 trained on `train_labels_v1`, which includes the gold studies with Gemini labels; heliosli R7 and S6fall membership unverified, assume all 4,407): any train-set prediction is in-sample and any gold-58 score is an upper bound of unknown looseness.

## 7. Limits

- n = 58 (macro SE about 0.017; MCL 9 positives). The headline table was chosen among about ten teacher variants on gold-58, so 0.920 is optimistic; the predeclared variant (`base_oof2_w30`, a-priori weight, both OOF families) is 0.915 [0.884, 0.941], +0.014 [−0.001, +0.028] vs `labels_v1_blend`. Dropping convnext is defensible a priori (it is the weaker family on report-fold OOF, 0.88 vs 0.90) but was confirmed on gold.
- The OOF teacher trained on `labels_v1_blend`; training a CoAtNet student on `base_own224_w30` is self-distillation. A label gain on gold-58 does not imply a model gain: the Oct 10 bootstrap-v1 label arm was mixed (+0.003 pooled, MCL −0.026).
- `labels_v1_blend`'s gold rows are reconstructed (R² 0.98–0.998), and its per-target weights may be gold-informed; steven_v4 is "gold-guided" per the Oct 8 note. Both baselines could be optimistic, which makes the +0.019 conservative rather than inflated.
- Gold-74's 16 extras are JEV's report-based verification; JEV tables are informed by them; the 16-row scores are descriptive.
- The Dread teacher and the A′ members are unscorable on gold; the silence evidence is gold-derived and must not be used to fit per-finding masks without acknowledging the circularity.
- No training, GPU, Kaggle, Colab or network use in this work; nothing committed; `STATUS.md` not updated by this lane.

**Recommendation for the fine-tuning lane:** train on `labels_v2_base_own224_w30.csv` (or `base_oof2_wfit.csv` as the predeclared-weight alternative), keep `labels_v2_combined.csv`'s `silent_*` flags available for a Synovitis/PF OA ablation, judge by report-fold OOF and LB, and treat gold-58 as spent for label selection.
