## Why this action

A learned joint localizer still needs qualified image-only localization evidence; the inspected knee tables have no spine-style landmarks. Training it now introduces annotation and validation work before testing the simplest available coverage alternative. Enlarging/recentering90mm against these images, or accepting partial central anatomy by renaming it a specialist, would rescue a failed gate after inspection. Multi-crop feature pooling would add another view/aggregation change and higher inference cost. The proposed single full-field comparison uses the already available field and original model, with one fixed endpoint and no fit or ensemble. A negative result would close this precise coverage hypothesis, not disprove localization or every frozen-feature method.
