# E4: second-stage per-finding stacking on clean OOF predictions, 2026-10-13

Zero-GPU analysis, no training of image models, no upload. Script `scripts/stacking_e4.py` (tests `tests/test_stacking_e4.py`, 6 synthetic
tests, including the leakage guards). Receipt and scores: `outputs/analysis/stacking-e4-20261013/` (`stacking_e4.json`, `scores.npz`, `run.log`).
Runtime 125 s on CPU.

## Setup

- Question: does a per-finding second-stage model on the 12 OOF outputs "decorrelate" compartment-confused findings and raise pooled masked macro AUC?
- Inputs: val rows of the five `oof_fold{k}.csv` of three clean own families, 4345 common studies, gold-58 rows dropped (`set == gold`):
  `own224` (`outputs/analysis/oof-coatnet224-r0`), `c384r` (CoAtNet-384 raw labels) and `r2c224` (CoAtNet-224 R2). The last two are the paths used by the
  OOF blend diagnostic (`docs/experiments/oof-blend-384-r2-20261011.md`). No nartaa Raptor or S6 output is used.
- Truth: `train_labels_v1` y_ >= 0.5 with m_ masks; AUC and masked macro (mean over defined targets) are the functions of `scripts/oof_blend_diagnostic.py`.
- Stacker: for target t, an L2 logistic regression (standardised logit inputs, all 12 findings of the family, 24 or 36 for multi-family) trained on rows with
  m_t = 1. **Nested CV over the existing folds**: fold k scores come from a stacker fitted only on the val-OOF rows of the other four folds. C is chosen from
  {0.003, 0.03, 0.3} by an inner leave-one-fold-out loop over those four folds only. Optional small GBM (depth 2, 120 trees, lr 0.04, L2 10, min leaf 40) on
  logits plus Lateral-Medial OA, Lateral-PF OA, Contusion-Fracture and Synovitis-Effusion differences. (Differences are linearly redundant for the LR.)
- Intervals: paired study-level bootstrap, 2000 draws, seed 42, stacked minus identity on the same resample. Folds are the exported grouped folds; no
  patient or report-component identifier was used, so a patient-clustered bootstrap could be slightly wider.
- Leakage guards (tests): the stacker's training rows never include the held-out fold and respect m_t; scores for fold k are bit-identical when fold-k
  labels and masks are scrambled; inner C selection is unchanged by held-out labels.

## Answers

**(a) Does stacking help macro?** No. With per-finding L2 LR the pooled masked macro is flat to slightly negative: own224 -0.0013 (CI -0.0027, +0.0000), blend2 -0.0007
(-0.0020, +0.0006), c384r+r2c224 inputs -0.0004 (-0.0017, +0.0008). The small GBM is significantly worse (-0.003 to -0.004). The one significant positive (all3 vs the 3-way mean, +0.0018) is
not a gain over the best identity (blend2, 0.9174; stacked all3 0.9166, delta -0.0009, CI -0.0021, +0.0004).

**(b) Which targets?** None reliably. Lateral OA (+0.0025 own224, +0.0044 blend2) and Lateral Meniscus (+0.0040 own224) have positive means with CIs spanning zero; PF OA, Fracture,
Contusion, Synovitis, Baker's and MCL lose, several with CIs excluding zero.

**(c) How much of the compartment confusion is linearly separable?** The prediction correlation is largely removable (Medial/Lateral OA 0.917 -> 0.776; Lateral/PF OA 0.914 -> 0.834), but
removing it does not buy discrimination: Lateral OA AUC moves 0.853 -> 0.856 and the others are unchanged or lower. Two things limit the headroom. First, the cross-compartment signal does carry
partner-specific information (Lateral OA is 0.814 when ranked by the Medial OA head, 0.853 by its own), but the stacker's gain over own is within noise. Second, the other outputs jointly
reproduce almost the full own-head AUC (Lateral OA 0.849 from the other 11 outputs), i.e. the heads share a latent severity/anatomy factor, and that shared part is not a nuisance a
linear map can subtract; what is left after fitting is image-level noise. Contusion/Fracture and Effusion/Synovitis correlations barely change because their label co-occurrence is also high.
The practical ceiling for stacked compartment fixes looks to be at most a few thousandths on Lateral OA (about 0.0004 macro), not the 0.03 gap seen with cross-ranking.

**(d) Recommendation: skip stacking** as a final step. It never beats the best identity blend, adds a fit-and-validate step, and a stacker fitted on single-fold OOF scores would be applied to averaged multi-model scores whose distribution
differs (not tested here). If compute allows, spend it on additional
diverse own members (the 2-family blend already gained +0.0043 over the best single family at essentially the cost of a second model) rather than a second stage. A per-target,
heavily shrunk Lateral OA/Lateral Meniscus term is the only candidate worth a re-test, and only on a fresh own-model OOF where it could be preregistered; selecting those two targets here
would be post-hoc. The improvement, if any, transfers only to own-model members, not to the exposed public arms.

## Limits

- Stacker features for training folds come from fold models that did see the held-out fold's images in training; this is the standard stacking convention and is not a leak of held-out labels
  into the held-out predictions, but it can make the stacker see slightly different score distributions than at test time.
- Single 5-fold partition; C grid and GBM settings were fixed before the run and not tuned further. Study-level bootstrap only.
- No gold-58 evaluation (too small to separate effects of this size).
