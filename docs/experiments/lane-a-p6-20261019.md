## P6s: shared decode (2026-10-20, reopened after Lane B's out-of-fold win)

Lane B measured dual − R6 flip at +0.0029 [+0.0019, +0.0039] out of fold. That reopened P6, with a pre-registered rule:

- Rehearsal point ≤ 26,000 s, with every other gate passing: submit P6s.
- Point in (26,000, 29,000] s: submit P6s′ (no R224) instead, under the same checks.

Implementation (commit f3322fd):

- The R6 plain production worker also loads the pinned R7 v1 fold models. They are checked against CPU state digests, compiled like `compact_infer --compile` (warm-up on 32–192 windows, then a compiled-vs-eager check on the first study at 1e-3) and scored right after R6's last fold on the same rendered windows. Each study is decoded once.
- R7 stackers are recomputed with `knee/public_r7_stackers.py`. The worker writes R7's own ledger, receipt and a flushed CSV, so salvage-before-constant still covers R7.
- Guard rule: near the deadline, R7 stops first. It stops when the remaining studies at the R6-only pace × 1.3 would pass T0 + 8.3 h. Studies without R7 keep rank(R6 **plain**) as their compact slot, because R6 flip is not run in P6s.
- New rehearsal gates: R6 and R7 must match the separate-pass P6 rehearsal on the frozen 39 within 1e-3 (the sentinel tolerance). R7 must also stay within 5e-3 of A-prime's R7.
