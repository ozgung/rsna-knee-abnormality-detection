## Pre-registration addendum: P9s shared decode (written 2026-10-20 16:15 UTC, before any P9s rehearsal)

- **What.** Variant `p9s` (`p9sr` with R224) has P9's arithmetic and member set; the members are rendered at 320 px inside the
  R6 plain worker's loader children from the same native cache R6 rendered at 384 px (one DICOM decode per study;
  `px_cache.StudySlices`, `probe_infer`, the anchor bank and `window_rows` are byte-identical in both trees), and scored
  by the main process right after R6's last fold with S6's own `compact_train.Net` (fp16, chunk 192, centre crop 288, the
  s6c worker's arithmetic). A study R6's image side check flipped is re-rendered for the members without the forced side,
  because the s6c path has no side model. There is no separate own stage; near the deadline the members stop first
  (R6-only pace × 1.3, the P6s rule) and those studies keep rank(R6 plain).
- **No flip TTA for members.** The S6 trainer (14767c7a) has no mirror augmentation, the members were fit with `--aug crop`,
  and their compact_infer (34f433fe) has no `--flip-tta`; flipping a canonically rendered knee would show the members an
  input they never saw. Flip TTA on members is therefore out of scope unless a mirror-augmented member is trained.
- **Gates (all pre-registered).** Every P9 gate; plus per member |Δp| ≤ 1e-3 on the frozen 39 against the P9 rehearsal's
  separate s6c worker (`P9S_SEPARATE_PASS_PARITY`); the R6 sentinel passes repeat the members (cross-process, cross-board
  ≤ 1e-3 per member; the injected sentinel of pass 1 is the only allowed failure there); the A100 fp16 reference ≤ 0.01;
  no cut, no fp32 rescue, no parameter mutation in the rehearsal. Projection: point ≤ 27,000 s, conservative 29,880 s gate
  with the same named waiver as P2 and P6s′ if only the projection fails.
- **Member budget rule (to be fixed from the P9s rehearsal, before any further member is added).** Marginal cost per member =
  the rehearsal's mean `member_seconds` per study scaled to 1,300 studies × the hidden window multiplier of the projection;
  members are added (in the order Lane L4/F exports arrive, all labels-v3 family or one later table, never mixed) while the
  projected point stays ≤ 27,000 s. The stride-2 timing proxy (member 0 on every other window) is recorded for information;
  production stays at eval stride 1 unless a stride-2 member set is rehearsed and gated on its own.
- **Reference.** `cfg['p9_reference']` = per-member production probabilities of `r6flip-p9-rehearsal-20261020` v1 on the
