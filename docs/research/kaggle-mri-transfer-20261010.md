# Transferable Kaggle MRI methods — October 10, 2026

**Recommendation:** finish the fixed public-model adaptation first; next qualify joint localization and test native ROI plus global context. Keep generic fold/backbone expansion held. The sources below support controlled hypotheses, not a known route to the current knee top ten.

Evidence checked October 10: targeted primary-source review of four RSNA 2024 lumbar-spine solution repositories and one RSNA-MICCAI 2021 brain-tumor repository. This is a bounded research brief, not an exhaustive review. Placement descriptions are the authors' repository claims; released components are not necessarily entire team solutions. No source experiment was reproduced.

## Past MRI evidence

- **Localization before classification:** [Brendan Artley's second-place component](https://github.com/brendanartley/RSNA-2024-Competition) describes coordinate-supervised disc crops, then CNN slice embeddings, LSTM and attention pooling. It uses sagittal T1/T2 differently for different findings. Its author also reports that an axial model improved personal CV but did not improve the team ensemble: another useful model need not be a useful addition. Pseudo-label mixing and disagreement-based label removal were used, but the README distinguishes the submitted loss from the released denoising implementation.
- **Physical crops and coordinate mapping:** [Moyasii's third-place repository](https://github.com/Moyasii/Kaggle-2024-RSNA-Pub) provides keypoint and classification stages. Its [dataset-construction code](https://github.com/Moyasii/Kaggle-2024-RSNA-Pub/blob/main/src/stage2/moyashii/tools/create_dastaset_v9.py) explicitly converts millimeter crop sizes through PixelSpacing and maps image/world coordinates. It also contains ground-truth coordinate fallback logic. These are inspected implementation facts, not evidence that copying its geometry or validation procedure would work for knees.
- **Separate localization and classification inputs:** [tattaka's fourth-place component](https://github.com/tattaka/rsna-2024-lumbar-spine-degenerative-classification-public) documents 256-pixel volumes for keypoints and original-resolution volumes for classification, using a lumbar coordinate dataset. This supports testing localization cheaply while retaining source samples for crops; the README does not establish an isolated resolution gain.
- **Anatomically matched feature fusion:** [lhwcv's seventh-place component](https://github.com/lhwcv/solution-rsna-2024-lumbar-spine/blob/main/solution.md) includes code concatenating sagittal and axial embeddings after level-specific cropping. Its alternative uses 3D keypoints and three/five-slice ROIs. The author identifies multiple axial series as a weakness and cautions that highest detection confidence need not select the best series.
- **A different MRI task:** [Rähmä AI's 2021 brain-tumor solution](https://github.com/jpjuvo/RSNA-MICCAI-Brain-Tumor-Classification) registers modalities, predicts segmentation, and selects tumor-region slices. No rank is asserted here. Its dependence on segmentation annotations and separately accepted Task-1 terms illustrates that localization supervision and data permission are prerequisites, not free architectural benefits. Brain registration is not evidence that knee sequences are already aligned.

## Knee evidence and transfer limits

Our [October 8 source audit](../experiments/20261008-community-followup.md) inspected the public CoAtNet's 96 slices, per-finding pooling and anatomical mirror, and the ConvNeXt reader's sequence/position tokens. Those notebook scores are dated observations; current pages did not render in this refresh. [October 9 participant reports](../experiments/top10-strategy-20261009.md) support testing soft image supervision but include a failure to transfer local gains to the leaderboard. They do not disclose current top-ten methods.

Today's project snapshot records best public 0.950 and own224 five-fold public 0.914; own384 fold report metrics near 0.913–0.914 are a different endpoint. These comparisons do not isolate representation, labels or geometry. The inspected knee CSV schemas contain study findings/reports and series descriptors, without spine-style landmark coordinates. A knee localizer therefore needs separately qualified image-only heuristics or training-fold annotations. Full-view context remains necessary for testing posterior/peripheral findings; homologous slice indices across planes cannot establish anatomical correspondence. Stored matrix size, PixelSpacing and enlargement do not prove intrinsic acquisition detail ([native-detail plan](../experiments/native-detail-roi-plan-20261010.md)).

## Prioritized experiments — proposals, not launches

1. **Inherited representation: retain the accepted experiment.** Compare the unchanged full public checkpoint against the exact two-epoch averaged endpoint, identical 94-window mirror evaluation. Use the existing [protocol](../experiments/public-adaptation-protocol-20261010.md), including its qualification gates and forgetting thresholds. Positive development change without prescribed damage is only a candidate signal. Nonpositive change does not justify expansion. Do not add distillation, crops or new labels mid-run.

2. **Native joint ROI with context.** Follow the existing matched comparison: 12 global plus 12 additional global views versus the same 12 global plus 12 native joint crops; identical encoder, initialization, labels and training/evaluation view budgets. Before fitting, audit a fixed nongold localization sample and record containment, failures and fallback coverage. Freeze acceptable coverage before outcomes. A later identical-box crop from the 336 cache distinguishes source-detail benefit from magnification. Require positive paired macro change and replication on a second fixed fold before expansion; reject systematic peripheral-target damage or unsafe localization.

3. **Useful coverage at fixed cost.** Separately compare the current min/max-span training-window sampling against deterministic plane/sequence/depth-stratified sampling, both 12 views, unchanged pooling and 94-window evaluation. Preserve missing-slot masks and short-series policy. Pass mechanics only with exact counts, reproducibility and ordering; seek paired target gains without macro harm. LSTM/ordered pooling would be a subsequent separate factor, not part of this sampler comparison.

4. **Sequence-aware fusion.** With fixed views and frozen encoder features, retrain both the existing per-finding pooling head and a compact sequence-token, masked fusion head, holding training labels, common-layer initialization and endpoint selection fixed; predeclare a comparable initialization policy for the two heads. Qualify missing-plane and duplicate-series handling first. Test anatomical matching only after image/world-coordinate checks; do not concatenate unregistered pixels. Require a positive paired macro change and second-fold confirmation before encoder fine-tuning or ensemble inclusion.

5. **Supervision refinement, later.** Compare original observed targets against one prospectively fixed soft-teacher policy, keeping initialization, images and optimization matched. For interpretable outer-fold evidence, create teachers entirely inside each outer training population; never generate validation truth from them. Global OOF assembly and the all-data public checkpoint cannot supply independent teachers. Keep unknown report cells masked unless that explicit training-only policy supplies pseudo-targets; reject disagreement-based deletion as an automatic truth rule. Require lineage PASS and replicated positive paired macro change without coherent target damage; otherwise stop expansion.

All new comparisons must predeclare sample, endpoint, runtime estimate and stop conditions. Report per-target support/AUC, undefined targets, unweighted defined-target macro and paired group-bootstrap uncertainty; existing gold and selected OOF remain development evidence. Intervals crossing zero remain inconclusive, even with positive point estimates. Audit external-weight/annotation permissions before new reuse. No training, data acquisition, terms acceptance or job changes were performed for this brief.
