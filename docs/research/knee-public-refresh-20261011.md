## Learned geometry and peripheral coverage

SKM-TEA V-Net `anatomy.pt` estimates joint/patella anchors from seven sagittal slices approximately4mm apart. Joint mask uses max femoral/tibial cartilage/meniscus probability>0.5, mass≥250 pixels; candidates must project within15–85% of source rows/columns, lie≤35mm from header slab prior and meet alignment checks (oblique cosine0.85). Candidates within20mm are mass-averaged; otherwise strongest wins. Failure uses header-derived orthogonal-stack slab prior. Anchors are projected through native patient coordinates and two-axis pixel spacing.

v4 preserves a full-field view and adds100mm square locals. Sagittal/axial locals cover joint, patellofemoral and posterior regions; coronal locals sit±30mm from joint. When patella segmentation is absent, sagittal PF offset is−25mm row/+40mm column and posterior+5/−35; axial PF−40/0 and posterior+35/0 in the canonical frame. These overlap and directly address the anterior clipping missed by our90mm center prior. The subsequent352/384 center crop reduces each nominal100mm local to about91.7mm of retained width. This is a different trained reader, not proof that our frozen checkpoint benefits from these inputs.

Inference reads images/headers only, rebuilds anchors, applies a learned side classifier and renders native pixels. Cache keys use SHA256 study/series strings truncated to24hex; temporary study directories use16hex. No training reports are required at inference.
