## 4. Provenance under the 743416 ruling

Ruling as relayed (Christopher Carr for RSNA, 2026-10-14 13:18 UTC, thread 743416; not readable by me): no per-dataset designations; external data must be publicly available and equally accessible at no cost; registration plus licence acknowledgement "clearly meets" this; an institutional research agreement, or access limited to people with an institutional affiliation, "clearly does not"; third-party weights are not addressed [literature, relayed].

- **fastMRI** (NYU): fetched today, `https://fastmri.med.nyu.edu/`: the application "must include the investigator's institutional affiliation and the proposed uses"; the individual signs electronically; no institutional signature; free; §5.3 bars redistribution of the dataset and "any subsequent variables or data files derived from" it, trained models not mentioned [measured fetch]. The affiliation field is a stated requirement, so fastMRI sits on the ruling's "access limited to people with an institutional affiliation" side, or at best in the grey zone the host leaves to "reasonableness and transparency" → **medium-to-high risk** [estimate]. §5.3 also leaves heliosli's redistribution of fastMRI-influenced weights unresolved (our Oct 13 note, unchanged).
