## 1. Critique of "a fine-tune beats its base and replaces it in the ensemble"

### 1.1 What dr-orig shows, and what it cannot

| Arm (fold 0, 870 studies, K42, masked report macro) | Init | Trained on | Macro | Exposure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dread v4_full (the base) | ImageNet → Dread's own recipe | all 4,349 non-gold studies, incl. the 870 | 0.9596 | fully exposed |
| dr-orig (Dread init + our recipe) | Dread v4_full backbone, fresh head | the other 3,475 | 0.9471 | exposed through the init |
| in-orig (same recipe from ImageNet) | ImageNet | the other 3,475 | 0.9068 | clean |

[measured: `raptor64-dread-init-protocol`, handoff 2026-10-13 18:12, it2 result]

- The 0.0125 drop is measured on studies whose labels the base memorised and the fine-tune never saw. It is consistent with *forgetting memorised labels* (neutral or good for generalisation) and with *degradation* (bad). It cannot discriminate, and no dev number from a knee-pretrained init can.
- It does bound plasticity: 12 epochs at bb_lr 3e-5 kept about 75 % of the exposed advantage (0.9471 − 0.9068 = +0.040 of the +0.053 the base holds over in-orig). The init's knowledge is neither preserved nor washed out; it is partly rewritten. That is the regime a new-view fine-tune needs (enough plasticity to learn the view), and the regime a same-view fine-tune should fear (rewriting with no new information).
- Health signals were clean: epoch-0 K16 0.9415, no target below own44 [measured]. A Dread or Raptor init on our pipeline does not collapse.
- dr-full (all 4,345 studies, SWA 9–11) has no development estimate at all [measured]; only the LB can read it.

Three same-view fine-tunes lost; the one gain came from new inputs, labels, data and schedule together. Coherent forgetting on rare targets (MCL 20 dev positives, Fracture 52) is the characteristic failure of the same-view case.

### 1.3 Why "beats its base" is the wrong test even when it is true

- **Ensemble value is not solo score.** A 0.929 reader at full weight lowered the 0.956 base [literature]; our 0.905–0.915 arms lowered Raptor's column at 0.3 because they were 0.86–0.89 correlated with it [measured, lane-2 diagnosis]. A fine-tune that is +0.002 solo but ρ ≈ 0.97 with its base adds less to the blend than a member −0.003 solo at ρ ≈ 0.85. R224 is the live example: 0.945 solo, same family, and worth only +0.0003 to +0.0008 in A′ [estimate from three public rank probes].
- **The public reading resolves 0.001, not less.** Paired SD of a one-arm change is 0.0004–0.0009 [measured, lane 3]; a true +0.0005 shows a displayed lead in 93 % of readings only together with ties. So the operational rule is tie-or-better (lane 3), not "beats". A fine-tune cannot be *confirmed* better than its base from one slot; it can only fail to be rejected.
- **Winner's curse.** Five slots a day invite probing several fine-tunes and keeping the best display; with ±0.001 noise that overfits the public set by roughly +0.0005–0.001 and the private rank pays for it. Pre-register the probes (Section 3) and keep them to two or three.
- **Dev is uninformative for knee-pretrained inits**, and gold-58 has per-target SE 0.04–0.07 [measured]. The only leak-robust signals are equal-exposure paired differences (Section 3.1) and the LB.

**Restated claim to work from:** a knee-pretrained model adapted to a new, anatomy-centred view, with the same or better labels, and placed in the slot of the ensemble member it is least correlated with, has a real chance of a +0.001 class gain; the same model re-trained on the view it already saw has an expected gain ≤ 0. The leaderboard is the test, under tie-or-better, with the swap pre-registered.

### 3.2 Pre-registered kill and promotion rules

- **K0 (cache).** Kill the cache (and rebuild with a different view) if the learned-anchor path is exercised on < 60 % of studies, or the field-centre fallback exceeds 30 % of sagittal series, or the 32-study montage shows a wrong-side/axis projection, or more than 2 of 32 sagittal locals cut the visible joint margin (the global R384 member keeps the context, so the M4 zero-truncation rule is relaxed to this explicit bound), or any axial PF local lacks the patella where the source shows it.
- **H (health).** Epoch-1 K16 < 0.85 for a knee-pretrained arm stops that arm (the dr-orig rule).
- **K1 (view effect, per init).** Primary contrast: Δ on the five localisation-sensitive targets (Lateral Meniscus, Lateral OA, PF OA, Medial OA, Medial Meniscus), local − global. Guards: macro Δ, and the six ceiling targets (ACL, Effusion, Synovitis, Baker's, Contusion, Fracture). **Kill** the view for that init if macro Δ < −0.010, or the five-target CI upper bound < 0. **Promote** to a full-data fit if five-target Δ ≥ +0.005 with CI lower > 0, macro Δ ≥ −0.005, and no ceiling target worse by > 0.02. ## 6. Limits

- Nothing in this document was executed; runtime, cache size and CPU-kernel speed are estimates built on 39-study rehearsal ledgers and T4-host preprocessing figures. The S6 preprocessing cost on a Kaggle CPU kernel is unmeasured.
- All P and E[Δ] figures are judgement; the one public data point for an anatomy-localised arm on top of the public base is one team's notebook (weights private) and one claimed +0.001.
- Every dev number for a knee-pretrained init is exposed, and the equal-exposure contrast has a known-direction bias against the new view of unknown size; one fold, one seed, no replication is planned before Oct 22.
- The frozen 12-epoch recipe was tuned for ImageNet inits (own224) and never for fine-tuning; nartaa's recipe is worth about +0.007 more at equal geometry [literature]. R1 inherits that gap; the LLRD factor is deferred to keep single-factor comparisons.
- Labels v2 and the ensemble-teacher pass are not available in time for the fold-0 pair; they are v2 factors or post-deadline work.
- The in-worker integration touches pinned heliosli modules and the frozen R7 worker; a parity failure there drops R1 regardless of its score.
- Hidden-run failures (6 of 18 submissions) and session speed variance (7.2–9.5 h conservative on identical bytes) are the dominant schedule risks; the chain has one day of slack.
- Licences: the nartaa weights card is "other" with an OAI acknowledgement; SKM-TEA anatomy weights are Apache-2.0 upstream; the compact bundle code is already in A. No new provenance exposure is added by R1/R2; R4 with S6fall adds CC BY-NC 4.0.
