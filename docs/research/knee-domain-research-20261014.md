## 0. Executive summary

- The three findings with the most recoverable headroom are **Lateral OA (0.860)**, **PF OA (0.864)** and **Lateral Meniscus (0.878)** [measured]. All three fail for the same structural reason: the 2.5D MIL model does not know *where* in the knee it is looking. The 0.91 prediction correlation between Medial and Lateral OA with much lower label co-occurrence [measured], and the fact that PF OA ignores the axial plane [measured], both point at missing anatomical localisation rather than missing capacity.
- Radiologically, medial vs lateral is a *position* (which end of the sagittal stack, which side of the coronal image), and patellofemoral cartilage is read on the *axial* plane [literature]. Both are cheap to give the model: a side-canonicalised slot order with compartment-aware attention masks, and a native-resolution axial anterior-crop arm.
- Effusion, Contusion, Fracture and Baker's are at or above their weak-label ceilings: public write-ups show image models beating the report labels on the 58 gold studies for exactly these four findings [literature]. Gains there will not show in fold-0 CV against masked weak labels, so they should not be chased this week.
- Localise-then-crop at native resolution won the last three RSNA challenges [literature]; nobody public in this competition has done anatomy-anchored crops yet, and the "heliosli anchored local-view" description could not be verified from any public text [literature]. The in-progress SKM-TEA ROI crop work is the right direction; its edge-containment problem is best solved with a fixed physical FOV rather than a tight bounding box.
- Ranked actions (section 6): (1) multi-teacher distillation that also fills masked labels, (2) side canonicalisation + compartment-aware pooling, (3) axial patellofemoral local-view arm, (4) fixed-FOV containment for the native-resolution crops, (5) two-slice-consistent meniscus pooling head.

## 1. Literature and public code: what works per finding, what data and weights exist

### 1.1 Architectures and input strategies

**2D-per-slice + pooling/attention beats 3D on anisotropic clinical series.** MRNet (Bien 2018): ImageNet AlexNet per slice, max over slices, one model per plane per task, logistic-regression fusion; AUC abnormal 0.937, ACL 0.965, meniscus 0.847 [literature] ([PLOS Med 2018](https://journals.plos.org/plosmedicine/article?id=10.1371/journal.pmed.1002699)). ELNet (Tsai 2020): tiny single-plane CNN with multi-slice normalisation, ACL 0.960 / meniscus 0.904 on MRNet, ACL 0.913 on KneeMRI [literature] ([MIDL 2020](http://proceedings.mlr.press/v121/tsai20a/tsai20a.pdf), [code](https://github.com/mxtsai/ELNet)). MRPyrNet (Dunnhofer 2021): feature pyramid + "pyramidal detail pooling" for small lesions, +2.2% ACL / +5.5% meniscus over MRNet [literature] ([MIDL 2021](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v143/dunnhofer21a.html), [code](https://github.com/matteo-dunnhofer/MRPyrNet)). Namiri 2020: 2D ≈ 3D for ACL on isotropic CUBE (accuracy 92% vs 89%, P = .27) [literature] ([Radiology: AI](https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/10.1148/ryai.2020190207)). Chang 2019: cropped FOV with 5-slice input beat 3- and 1-slice (0.915 vs 0.865 vs 0.765), best test AUC 0.971 [literature] ([J Digit Imaging](https://www.doi.org/10.1007/S10278-019-00193-4)). Yiu 2025: ResNet50 transfer learning beat ViTs on MRNet [literature] ([arXiv 2508.14151](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.14151v2)). Takeaway: our 2.5D 3-slice-window CoAtNet with per-finding attention MIL is the right family; literature gains came from *cropping and slice context*, not from 3D nets.

**Multi-plane fusion helps ACL and meniscus modestly.** MPFuseNet (Belton 2021): ACL 0.977 / abnormal 0.957 at 3x training cost [literature] ([arXiv 2108.08136](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.08136)). A 2026 hybrid-fusion study found three-plane fusion for ACL (0.949) *below* the best single plane, sagittal (0.976), while for meniscus axial (0.826) beat sagittal (0.791) [literature] ([Dergipark](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/jai/article/1926635)). Slice-level attention with Bi-GRU continuity modelling gave the largest gain for ACL [literature] ([Waseda 2026 thesis](https://www.ams.giti.waseda.ac.jp/data/pdf-files/2026_kunitomi_mt.pdf)). This supports the brief's observation that the sagittal-dominant model is near the ACL ceiling and that a plane-aware head token was null [measured].

**Localise-then-classify is the consistent winner for small structures.** Liu 2019: 3-CNN cascade (slice selection, YOLO ACL isolation, DenseNet), ACL AUC 0.98 [literature] ([Radiology: AI](https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/full/10.1148/ryai.2019180091)). Astuto 2021: ROI detection then per-tissue grading, AUC 0.83-0.93 on 1,435 CUBE studies [literature] ([Radiology: AI](https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/10.1148/ryai.2021200165)). Rizk 2021: localisation + classification 3D CNN on 11,353 exams, medial meniscus 0.93, lateral 0.84 [literature] ([Physica Medica](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1120179721000983)). Tack 2021: multi-task 3D CNN with bounding-box regressor on 2,399 OAI DESS, AUC MM 0.93-0.94, LM 0.91-0.96 [literature] ([Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/bioengineering-and-biotechnology/articles/10.3389/fbioe.2021.747217/full)). RSNA 2022 cervical spine 1st place: 3D U-Net vertebra segmentation from 87 masks, per-vertebra crops, 2.5D CNN + LSTM [literature] ([discussion](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/rsna-2022-cervical-spine-fracture-detection/discussion/362787)); RSNA 2024 lumbar spine top solutions: keypoint localisation, fixed-size crops per level, 2.5D classifiers, sagittal/axial fusion [literature] ([2nd place](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/rsna-2024-lumbar-spine-degenerative-classification/writeups/ianpan-kevin-yuji-bartley-2nd-place-solution), [Zenn summary](https://zenn.dev/mkj/articles/ba630a8837ee72)).

**Lateral meniscus is harder for everyone.** Fritz 2020 (arthroscopy reference): DCNN sensitivity 84% medial vs 58% lateral, AUC 0.882 vs 0.781; radiologists 93-96% vs 67-71% [literature] ([Skeletal Radiol](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00256-020-03410-2)). A distillation study: 0.834 medial vs 0.746 lateral [literature] ([Frontiers 2023](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/bioengineering-and-biotechnology/articles/10.3389/fbioe.2023.1326706/pdf)). Our gap (0.942 vs 0.878) [measured] matches the literature pattern, so part is irreducible, but Tack's result (LM ≥ MM once horns are localised) [literature] says localisation closes much of it.

**Effusion, BML and cysts.** DL effusion-synovitis work is all 2D sagittal fluid-sensitive on OAI with moderate agreement to MOAKS (Dice 0.79, rho 0.65) [literature] ([eScholarship](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/7294c75t), [Felfeliyan](https://www.arxiv.org/pdf/2107.12889)). BML on clinical MRI: accuracy 81%, sensitivity 93%, specificity 79% [literature] ([PMC10298632](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10298632/)); OAI DESS weakly supervised with MOAKS labels, AUC 0.85-0.93 [literature] ([ISMRM 2021](https://cds.ismrm.org/protected/21MProceedings/PDFfiles/4062.html)). Baker's cyst: one proof-of-concept SE-YOLOv5 on 282 patients, mAP 0.944, separating cysts from effusions [literature] ([PMC9397605](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9397605/)). No DL paper separates contusion from fracture on knee MRI. No MCL-specific DL paper beyond a 60-patient slice-level VGG19 study [literature] ([PubMed 41238423](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41238423/)).

1. **PF OA** has the cleanest headroom [estimate]: large gap, 169 positives (a +0.02 change is detectable), label ceiling 0.90, a known untapped plane, and a clear radiological mechanism.
2. **Lateral OA** has the largest nominal gap but the noisiest labels and only 75 positives. The 0.91 correlation with Medial OA and the positive sagittal-drop ablation [measured] say the model lacks compartment localisation; the fix is shared with Lateral Meniscus.
3. **Lateral Meniscus** is clinically the hardest of the 12, so part of the gap is irreducible, but localisation recovers much of it in the literature.
4. **MCL**: SE 0.048; single-fold changes are noise. Protect it, do not select on it.
5. **Effusion, Contusion, Fracture, Baker's**: image models already exceed report labels on gold [literature]; fold-0 AUC is capped by label noise. Poor targets for pre-registered tests.
6. **Synovitis**: the observed 440 is a biased subset; the 430 masked studies are where test errors live; distillation is the only lever.
7. **ACL, MM, Medial OA** are within 0.01 of their label ceilings.

Macro arithmetic [estimate]: closing one third of the gaps on PF OA, Lateral OA and Lateral Meniscus is worth ≈+0.007 macro, about the whole in-orig to in-distil difference [measured].

## 4. Low-hanging fruit under the constraints

Zero inference cost: side canonicalisation (a flip and a slot reorder), compartment attention masks, a different pooling head, distillation targets, mirror augmentation in training. Cheap at inference: an axial-only arm (12-24 windows per study on a ConvNeXt-T is minutes for 1,300 studies on one T4 [estimate]). Do not start now: 3D nets, fastMRI+ detector training, OAI access, paired-sequence inputs, LLM relabelling (null already [measured]).

The SKM-TEA segmenter already in the repo yields three free by-products: a side oracle (medial vs lateral tibial cartilage classes), a joint centre (cartilage centroid) for fixed-FOV crops, and a notch centre for ACL crops. These enable items 2, 3 and 4.

## 6. Actionable items, ranked by expected gain per day

Common protocol: single-factor change on top of in-distil, fold-0 (870 studies), K42, truth = masked weak labels, one seed. Kill rules are fixed before training. Cost unit **R** = one fold-0 in-distil run (12 epochs, K8, CoAtNet-384); I estimate R ≈ 1-3 A100-hours [estimate].

### Item 1. Multi-teacher distillation that also fills masked labels
- **Change**: average two or three public image-only teachers (nartaa OAI-trained CoAtNet SWA, dreaddevelopment Fine-Spacing CoAtNet, goodpjw2008 ConvNeXt reader); keep 0.5 soft-label weight on observed entries; **for masked entries use the teacher average as the sole target at weight 0.5** instead of masking. Targets: Synovitis, Baker's, Lateral OA, PF OA.
- **Why**: in-distil was the largest measured gain (+0.008 macro) [measured]; masked entries are where test-time Synovitis errors live; nartaa's teacher carries OAI knowledge we cannot obtain directly [literature].
- **Test and kill rule**: fold-0 vs in-distil. Kill if macro < +0.002 or any of ACL/MM/Medial OA drops > 0.01. Synovitis on the observed 440 must not drop.
- **Cost**: teacher inference over 4,345 studies once (≈1-2 A100-hours [estimate]); then 1R. Inference: **zero**.
- **Expected gain**: +0.002-0.004 macro solo [estimate]; smaller in the final blend.

### Item 2. Side canonicalisation + compartment-aware pooling
- **Change**: (a) derive the medial direction per study: check `Laterality`/`ImageLaterality`; otherwise take the end of the IPP-sorted sagittal stack where SKM-TEA medial-tibial-cartilage voxels dominate (medial and lateral counts must cross once along the stack). (b) Canonicalise: sagittal slots always run lateral → medial; coronal/axial flipped in-plane so medial is always on the same side. (c) Compartment masks on per-finding attention: MM, Medial OA, MCL attend to medial-half sagittal + all coronal/axial; LM, Lateral OA to lateral-half sagittal + all coronal/axial; other seven unmasked; implement as a learned soft bias. (d) With canonical sides, mirror augmentation **without label swap** is correct (a mirrored right knee is a left knee with the same findings); add as a second stage with mirror TTA.
- **Why**: medial vs lateral is purely positional for a radiologist [literature]; the model confuses compartments [measured]; medial tears leak into lateral predictions publicly [literature]; the earlier mirror experiment swapped labels, which is the wrong inverse [measured]; nartaa's 0.949 uses anatomical mirror TTA [literature].
- **Test and kill rule**: stage A (canonicalisation only) then stage B (masks). Kill B if mean AUC over {LM, Lateral OA, Medial OA, MM} < +0.005 over A, or macro < -0.002. Kill A if macro < -0.002. Side oracle must agree with the DICOM tag on ≥98% of studies where it exists.
- **Cost**: oracle ≈1-2 A100-hours if segmentations are not cached; 2R. Inference: **zero** (flip and reorder are free; segmenter already runs for ROI crops). Mirror TTA doubles only our own arm.
- **Expected gain**: LM +0.01-0.02, Lateral OA +0.01-0.02, Medial OA +0.005; macro +0.003-0.005 [estimate].

### Item 3. Axial patellofemoral local-view arm
- **Change**: a second small model (ConvNeXt-T or CoAtNet-1 at 384) on native-resolution crops of the **anterior half of the axial PD FS series** (anterior 55% × central 65% of the 140 mm field is robust), 3-slice windows over all axial slices (≤24), per-finding heads for all 12 but blended only into PF OA, Effusion and Synovitis at a **fixed** rank-blend weight of 0.3.
- **Why**: PF OA ignores axial [measured] although axial is the plane for patellar cartilage [literature]; patellar cartilage at 384 px over 140 mm is ~6 px, unresolvable for Outerbridge grading; the suprapatellar recess and Hoffa's pad sit in the same crop; OAI-trained public arms cannot have learned this (OAI has no native axial fluid-sensitive series [literature]), so the arm adds blend diversity.
- **Test and kill rule**: fold-0, blended at 0.3 vs in-distil alone. Kill if PF OA < +0.010, or Effusion or Synovitis drops > 0.005. No weight tuning on fold-0.
- **Cost**: crop cache ≈0.5 A100-hour; training ≈2-4 A100-hours [estimate]. Inference: ≈30k window forwards at 384 px fp16 on one T4, ≈4-8 min [estimate].
- **Expected gain**: PF OA +0.015-0.03, Effusion +0.003, Synovitis +0.003; macro +0.002-0.003 [estimate].

### Item 4. Fixed physical FOV for the in-progress native-resolution crops
- **Change**: replace the tight segmentation box with a fixed physical window (120 mm sagittal centred on the SKM-TEA cartilage centroid; 140 mm coronal so the distal MCL stays inside) at native pixel pitch; never let the crop touch the medial border. Targets: ACL, LM, MM, MCL.
- **Why**: containment is the reported blocker [measured]; a 1.5x zoom cost 0.018 mostly on MCL and wide-FOV scored below the 140 mm crop [literature]; RSNA 2022-2024 winners used fixed-size crops around a localised anchor [literature]; nartaa uses a 320 px native centre crop [literature].
- **Test and kill rule**: fold-0, crops replacing current sagittal/coronal windows. Kill if macro < +0.002 or MCL < -0.02 (containment canary).
- **Cost**: cache ≈1-2 A100-hours; 1R. Inference: neutral if crops replace resized windows.
- **Expected gain**: ACL +0.005-0.01, LM +0.005; macro +0.001-0.002 [estimate].

### Item 5. Two-slice-consistent pooling head for the menisci
- **Change**: an instance-logit branch for MM and LM whose study score is max over adjacent sagittal window pairs of min(logit_i, logit_i+1), averaged with the attention-pooled logit; check on frozen features first.
- **Why**: the clinical tear criterion requires surface-contacting signal on two adjacent slices (PPV 94% vs 43%) [literature]; attention MIL has no adjacency prior; continuity modelling gave the largest MRNet gains [literature].
- **Test and kill rule**: fold-0 vs in-distil. Kill if LM + MM mean < +0.004 or macro < 0.
- **Cost**: frozen-feature version ≈0.5 A100-hour; full version 1R. Inference: zero.
- **Expected gain**: LM +0.005, MM +0.002; macro +0.0005-0.001 [estimate]. Lowest confidence.

### Sequencing for 8 days
Day 1: item 1 teacher inference + item 2 side oracle (offline preprocessing). Day 2: item 1 (1R) and item 2 stage A (1R) in parallel. Day 3: item 2 stage B and item 3 training. Days 4-5: read-outs, item 4 cache + run. Day 6: combine survivors in one run; verify on fold-0 that gains are at least 50% additive. Day 7: full-data training of the survivor recipe; measure inference time on T4. Day 8: submission and buffer. Do not select on the 58 gold studies (SE ≈0.02 [literature]).
